## A place for church-owned updates

This news space gives First Christian Church Anniston a first-party place for stories, service updates, ministry notes, and reflections. Facebook can still carry quick announcements, but durable church news should also live on a page the church controls.

## What belongs here

- Sunday and seasonal schedule notes
- Ministry stories and photos approved for public sharing
- Pastoral reflections
- Community invitations
- Volunteer opportunities

Posts here are written in Markdown and reviewed through the same publishing workflow that deploys the public website.
